About the campus

WHERE 8TH MILE LIVES.

RVCE is one of Bengaluru’s oldest engineering colleges and home to 8th Mile, a two-day fest that draws students from across South India. The campus hosts technical events in its labs, cultural shows on the main stage and sports on the open grounds off Mysore Road.
