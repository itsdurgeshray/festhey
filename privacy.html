01Introduction

Welcome to FestHey. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have questions or concerns about this notice or how we handle your information, please contact us.

02Information we collect

We collect personal information you give us when you register, show interest in our products and services, take part in activities on the website or contact us.

Personal detailsName, email and phone number
Payment dataProcessed by secure third-party gateways
Social loginBasic profile from the provider you choose

03How we use your information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, to fulfil our contract with you, with your consent, or to meet legal obligations.

  • To create your account and log you in
  • To post testimonials and collect feedback
  • To manage your account and registrations
  • To send you administrative information

04Data security

We protect your personal information with organisational and technical security measures. These are designed to keep any information we process safe, but no online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
