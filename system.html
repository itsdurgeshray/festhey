06 Texture and motion

A fine grain sits over the whole page and a heavier grain over the orange and yellow sections. Real-world objects (lanyards, phones, hands, paper, tape, pegs) are photographed cut-outs, never illustrations of them. Motion is physical: things hang, swing and settle with springs; nothing slides in without a reason.
