About FestHey

THE HEART OF CAMPUS

India’s event discovery platform for college fests, bridging the gap between organisers and the students who make every fest come alive.

Who we are

Founded in 2024, FestHey started as a small project to fix the chaos of fest registrations in a few local colleges. Today we connect thousands of students across the country to the fests they love.

Our story

FROM GROUP CHATS TO ONE TAP

Fest registrations used to live in spreadsheets, payment screenshots and forwarded forms. We built FestHey so students could find every fest in one place, register in a tap, and bring their whole team along.
