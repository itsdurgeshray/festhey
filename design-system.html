01 Foundations

Principles

Four rules every screen follows. If a choice breaks one, it is the wrong choice.

Say it in a second

Lead with what FestHey is: college fests, all in one place. The headline and first image must explain the product without scrolling.

Real things, real people

Use photographed cut-outs of real objects (lanyards, phones, hands, tape, paper) and real campus photos. Never draw what can be photographed.

Loud colour, calm layout

Orange and sun carry the energy. Layouts stay simple: one message and one action per section, clean grids, generous space.

Everything on the 2px grid

Every size, space, radius and border is a multiple of 2. Line height is the font size plus 8 or plus 16.

06 Foundations

18 Content

Copy rules

Short, energetic, student-first. These hold on every page.

Do

  • Use the client's words for fests, events and FAQs
  • Sentence case for body; caps only for H2 and eyebrows
  • Prices as ₹349.00 / Member, dates as Dec 5 to Dec 6
  • One highlighted phrase per heading at most

Don't

  • Em or en dashes anywhere in visible copy
  • Italics
  • Decorative text scattered behind sections
  • Outline-only buttons
