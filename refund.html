REFUND POLICY

Last updated May 26, 2026 · 4 sections · 4 min read

The short version

Each organiser sets the refund rules for their own event.

Cancel from My Registrations; we handle the rest.

Approved refunds land in 5 to 7 business days.

On this page

01General refund terms02Cancellation by the organiser03Cancelling it yourself04Processing time

01General refund terms

FestHey provides the infrastructure for event registration and payment. Every event on the platform may have its own refund policy, and it is your responsibility to review those terms before you pay.

Refund rules are set by the individual event organisers. FestHey facilitates the process but does not set the rules for every event.

02Cancellation by the organiser

If an organiser cancels an event, you are entitled to a full refund of the registration fee. FestHey works with the organiser to start that refund automatically; you don’t need to do anything.

03Cancelling it yourself

To cancel, go to My Registrations in your dashboard. Whether you get money back depends on when you cancel:

Full refund100%

Cancelled within the organiser’s free cancellation window.

Partial refundVaries

After the free window, before the event’s refund deadline.

No refund0%

After the refund deadline, or once the event has started.

04Processing time

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Allow 5 to 7 business days for the amount to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

  1. RequestCancel in My Registrations
  2. ApprovalOrganiser reviews, usually within 48 hours
  3. Refund5 to 7 business days to your account
